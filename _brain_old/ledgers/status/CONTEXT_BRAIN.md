1.  **STRATEGY:** 5 Venture Briefs (The Soul, Market, People, Pull, Proof).
2.  **LANDSCAPE:** Visual Heuristics (Multimodal analysis of competitor conventions).
3.  **JOURNEY:** Action Strategy (Architecting Use Cases before drawing flows).
4.  **STRUCTURE:** OOUX (Defining stable objects/nouns before screens).
5.  **WIREFRAME:** Atomic rendering of strategic intent.
