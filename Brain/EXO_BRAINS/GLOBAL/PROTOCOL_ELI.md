# THE ELI PROTOCOL
*Mandatory structure for every strategic argument.*

- **[E]vidence:** Cite a real fact, product, user behavior, or market data point. Do not assert without evidence.
- **[L]ogic:** Explain why this evidence matters for THIS specific product. Connect it to the insight.
- **[I]mplication:** State what the NEXT team must do with this. Produce a direction, not just an observation.
