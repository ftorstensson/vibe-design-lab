# RESEARCH RULES — SHARED AGENT PROTOCOL (v1.2)
*How to search, what to trust, and when to stop.*

## 1. THE INTERNAL LOOP (VOLUME)
- **Broad Scanning:** You have permission to scan up to 50 sources internally to find the scent of the truth.
- **Intent-First:** Before searching, state your specific question. "Search for MyFitnessPal competitors" is bad. "Search for the top 3 MyFitnessPal rivals by active user growth in 2025" is venture-grade.

## 2. THE TRUST FILTER (NUANCE)
- **Primary Over Secondary:** SEC filings, App Store data, and academic papers (PubMed/JSTOR) are the gold standard for Facts.
- **The Expert Loophole:** Blogs and industry newsletters are valid if they provide **Strategic Logic** or **Contrarian Insights** that official reports ignore. Cite them as "Expert Commentary."
- **Recency as a Variable:** Market data must be < 24 months old. Behavioral psychology (Bartle types, Hook model) is evergreen—a 10-year-old foundational study beats a recent summary.

## 3. THE DISTILLATION RULE (UX)
- **The Sacred 3-5:** Never overwhelm the Director with link-spam. Distill your internal findings into the 3-5 most load-bearing sources.
- **Citation Format:** Every major claim must follow: claim [Source Name, URL].
- **Conflict Handling:** If sources disagree (e.g., conflicting market size), do not average them. Present both extremes with their respective URLs and let the Synthesizer adjudicate.

## 4. STOP CONDITIONS
- **The 3-Attempt Law:** If after 3 distinct search strategies you cannot verify a claim, it is an "Unverified Assumption." Do not hallucinate a source to fill the gap. Flag it explicitly.
