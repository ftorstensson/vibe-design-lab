# PROJECT BRAIN: VIBE DESIGN LAB
*Current Status: V2.0 (Executive Strategy & Sequential Workflow)*
*Last Updated: 2026-01-08*

## 1. THE MISSION
Transitioning from a prototype "Map" to a premium **"Strategy Suite."** The system now authors 8 distinct **Executive Position Papers** that serve as the immutable DNA for all downstream design decisions.

## 2. THE 95/5 DOCTRINE
- **95% Convention:** Agents must prioritize established category norms to reduce user cognitive load.
- **5% Differentiation:** Intentional, strategic deviation to create market "Oxygen."

## 3. THE STRATEGY ARTEFACTS (The 8 Papers)
1. **Product Intent, Market & Success:** Why we exist + Competitive failures.
2. **Audience & Buyer Type:** Achievers, Creators, Explorers, Socialisers.
3. **Convention & Category Norms:** Establishing the baseline "95%".
4. **Value Prop & Content Spine:** Ensuring everything has a job.
5. **Experience Principles:** Aligning taste and interaction guardrails.
6. **Functional Scope & Boundaries:** MUST-HAVE vs. NON-GOALS.
7. **Content, IA & Discoverability:** Making the system legible.
8. **Lifecycle & Growth Readiness:** AARRR behavioral intent (Acquisition to Relationship).

## 4. MISSION V2.0 GOALS
- **Sequential Printing:** PM authors one paper at a time. User must approve/refine before moving to the next.
- **Executive Aesthetic:** Replace 8-bit nodes with "Executive Papers" (Superior typography, context-first).
- **Interactive Deep-Dives:** Ability to "Interview" the specific department owner of each paper.
